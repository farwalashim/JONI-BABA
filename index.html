The Artisan Profile

Master of Craft since 2014

The Soul Behind Joni Baba Store

My journey into leather crafting began in 2014. What started as a small personal passion for handmade goods soon became a lifelong commitment to quality and tradition in the heart of Multan.

Click to read our full story & experience ↓

For more than a decade, I have focused on 'Saddle Stitching'—a traditional hand-sewing technique that is impossible for machines to replicate. Every item at Joni Baba Store is hand-selected and hand-stitched to ensure it carries the spirit of true craftsmanship.

Today, we stand as a symbol of heritage in Pakistan. Our goal is to provide goods that don't just age, but develop a beautiful story with every year you use them.

Expertise: Hand-Stitching, Patina Art, Bespoke Leatherwork.
